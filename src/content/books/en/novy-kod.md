The books about food I needed at thirty-five didn't exist. The ones I found at fifty weren't written for me. This one I wrote first for the quiet men with rules they had never named — and then for anyone who is tired of the loud version of this story.

There are no weight figures here, no before-and-after stories, no calories, no plan. It makes no promise of transformation. What it offers is mostly company.
