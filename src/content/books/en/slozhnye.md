The books about food I needed earlier didn't exist. This one I wrote about the three things that always come with food — weight, love, and beauty — and the rules we received without choosing them.

There are no weight figures here, no before-and-after stories, no calories, no plan. It makes no promise of transformation. What it offers is mostly company.
